A 20-second vertical GTA 6 clip for the make.fun bounty "Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views". The hook is the opening moment of Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2: the Leonida Keys aerial, Jason on the roof, and Jason driving — cut to 9:16 (1080x1920), with "GTA 6 IS HERE" over the first seconds. After the hook, the clip pitches make.fun on screen: "Make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun" (make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01). The clip goes up as a public Short on the CandyDAO YouTube channel; VIDEO.json names the video once it is uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
